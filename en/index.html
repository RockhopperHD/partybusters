      How to Play

      Your goal is to correctly ADMIT or REJECT guests based on the level's rules.

      Listen to what the guest says. Open the rule clipboard (hover over 'RULES') to see the rules. For EACH rule, decide if the guest Follows (✅) or Breaks (❌) it using the buttons.

      You must assess ALL rules before making a decision. When done, the Admit and Reject buttons on the clipboard will activate.

      Click Admit if the guest follows ALL rules (you marked ✅ for all). Click Reject if they break ANY rule (you marked ❌ for at least one).

      Correct decisions earn points and progress towards the quota. Incorrect decisions cost a life (❤️). Running out of time also costs a life.

      At the end of the level, the representative might ask a quick practice question. Answer it to continue!
