# Partybusters!
Partybusters! (also called ¡Guardafiesta! in Spanish and other titles in its French and Italian version) is a simple HTML game about managing a birthday party and language learning. It's infinitely expandable using AI tools, and it's meant for teachers and students looking to review with very minimal setup.
## The Game
In Partybusters! students play as a security guard managing a birthday party. They are given rules at the start of the level, and then are presented with various characters which they then admit or reject from the party based on whether or not they follow the rules.

Rules can be simple, like “don’t let anyone in if they like pizza” — or they can be complex, like “everyone needs a gift unless they’re a VIP.”

The game starts easy with one simple rule. Each level adds one (up to three) before changing them to complex rules. Full difficulty is at level 6.

## Expanding it with a Chatbot
The game comes with a simple set of partygoers and rules, however a teacher (or a student looking to review) can generate more content for the game in minutes. It comes with an import sheet: a special Google Doc and educator can fill out and upload to Google Gemini (a free AI tool!) to instantly make more content for the game. To access the import sheet, click the link on the title screen.
